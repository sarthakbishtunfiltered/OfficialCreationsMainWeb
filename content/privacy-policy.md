# Privacy Policy

**Last updated: November 15, 2026**

Official Creations ("OC", "we", "us", or "our") is committed to respecting and protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, store, disclose and protect personal information when you visit our websites, use our platforms, purchase our services or products, create an account, or communicate with us.

This policy applies to all current and future Official Creations websites, platforms, products, applications and services.

Please read this Privacy Policy carefully. By using our services, you acknowledge the practices described in this policy, subject to any consent required by applicable law.

## 1. Information We Collect

We may collect the following categories of information, depending on how you interact with OC.

**Information you provide:**

- Name, email address and phone number.
- Business name, industry, website and business details.
- Information submitted through forms, consultations, applications and enquiries.
- Account registration details and preferences.
- Information required to deliver our services and products.
- Communications and other information you voluntarily provide.

**Automatically collected information:**

When you visit our websites or use our platforms, we may collect technical and usage information, including:

- IP address and approximate location.
- Browser, device and operating system information.
- Pages visited and interactions with our platforms.
- Usage patterns, preferences and activity history.
- Referral sources and campaign information.

**Payment information:**

Payments may be processed through third-party payment providers. OC does not intend to store complete payment card or banking credentials. Payment providers handle such information according to their own privacy and security practices.

## 2. How We Use Your Information

We may use collected information to:

- Provide, operate and maintain our services and products.
- Create and manage user accounts.
- Respond to enquiries and provide customer support.
- Process bookings, payments and service requests.
- Send service-related notifications and updates.
- Send marketing communications, offers and announcements where permitted.
- Improve our websites, products and services.
- Analyse usage and understand customer preferences.
- Measure advertising and marketing performance.
- Develop and operate automated and AI-powered features.
- Detect fraud, prevent misuse and protect our platforms.
- Meet applicable legal and regulatory requirements.

We may also use anonymised and aggregated information for analytics, research and service improvement.

## 3. Communications

We may communicate with users through email, SMS, WhatsApp or other available communication channels.

These communications may include:

- Account and service notifications.
- Booking confirmations and reminders.
- Project updates and customer support.
- Product announcements and promotional offers.
- Newsletters and marketing communications.

Where required, we will obtain appropriate consent before sending promotional communications.

You may opt out of marketing communications using the unsubscribe option provided or by contacting us.

Opting out of promotional messages will not prevent us from sending essential service-related communications where necessary.

## 4. Sharing Your Information

We do not sell your personal information to third parties.

We may share information in the following circumstances:

**Service providers:** We may share necessary information with external providers that help us operate our websites, platforms and services. These may include hosting providers, payment processors, analytics services, communication platforms, CRM systems, automation tools and AI service providers.

**Advertising and analytics:** We may use advertising and analytics technologies to understand website activity, measure campaigns and improve our marketing.

**Legal requirements:** We may disclose information when required by applicable law, court orders or lawful government requests.

**Protection of rights:** We may disclose information when reasonably necessary to protect our rights, users, property, security or the public.

**Business transfers:** Information may be transferred as part of a merger, acquisition, restructuring or transfer of business operations, subject to applicable law.

We expect relevant service providers to handle information appropriately and use it only for authorised purposes.

## 5. Cookies and Tracking Technologies

Our websites and platforms may use cookies, pixels and similar technologies to improve functionality and understand user activity.

These technologies may be used for:

- Essential website functionality.
- Website analytics and performance measurement.
- Advertising and campaign measurement.
- Understanding visitor interactions and preferences.

We may introduce or change analytics and advertising tools as our services develop.

You can manage cookies through available website privacy settings or your browser settings. Where required by law, we will request consent before using optional tracking technologies.

Disabling certain cookies may affect some website features.

## 6. Your Privacy Rights

Depending on your location and applicable privacy laws, you may have the right to:

- **Access:** Request a copy of the personal information we hold about you.
- **Correction:** Request correction of inaccurate or incomplete information.
- **Deletion:** Request deletion of your personal information, subject to legal requirements.
- **Withdraw consent:** Withdraw consent where processing is based on your consent.
- **Opt out:** Unsubscribe from marketing communications and exercise applicable advertising or data-sharing opt-out rights.
- **Restrict processing:** Request restrictions on certain types of processing where applicable.
- **Data portability:** Request a portable copy of your information where required by law.
- **Object:** Object to certain processing activities where applicable.

To exercise these rights, contact us using the official contact details provided in Section 14.

We may need to verify your identity before processing a request. Certain information may need to be retained where required by law or for legitimate business purposes.

We will not unlawfully discriminate against individuals for exercising their applicable privacy rights.

## 7. AI and Automated Technologies

OC may use artificial intelligence and automated technologies across its services, products and internal operations.

These technologies may help us:

- Improve customer experiences.
- Automate business processes.
- Analyse information and usage patterns.
- Provide personalised features and recommendations.
- Operate AI-powered products and services.

Depending on the feature, information submitted by users may be processed through third-party AI or automation providers.

We aim to use such technologies responsibly and in accordance with applicable privacy requirements.

## 8. International Data Transfers

OC may work with service providers and technology platforms operating in different countries.

As a result, personal information may be processed or transferred outside your country of residence, including to countries where our service providers maintain their infrastructure.

Where required by applicable law, we will use appropriate safeguards for international transfers.

## 9. Data Retention

We retain personal information only for as long as reasonably necessary for the purposes described in this Privacy Policy.

Retention periods may depend on:

- The nature of the information.
- The services or products being provided.
- Account and business requirements.
- Legal, accounting and regulatory obligations.
- Dispute resolution and security requirements.

When information is no longer required, we will take reasonable steps to delete or anonymise it.

## 10. Children and Age Restrictions

Our websites may be accessible to individuals of different ages.

However, purchases, subscriptions and contractual services generally require individuals to be at least 18 years old or to have appropriate involvement and authorisation from a parent or legal guardian where permitted by law.

We do not knowingly collect personal information from children in violation of applicable privacy laws.

If you believe a child has provided personal information inappropriately, please contact us so that we can review the matter.

We implement reasonable security measures to help protect personal information against unauthorised access, alteration, disclosure or destruction.

However, no method of electronic transmission or storage is completely secure. We cannot guarantee absolute security.

If a personal data breach occurs, we will take appropriate steps to investigate and address it and notify affected individuals or authorities where required by applicable law.
